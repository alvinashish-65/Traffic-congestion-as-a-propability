Signalised approach: Webster · Harahap eq. 3.7

One lane with Poisson arrivals. Red is 153 s, then green 90 s, then yellow 10 s. Cars leave only on green, one every 0.857 s, so capacity is 0.415 veh/s. The black line is the simulated mean wait, and its 95% CI band is narrower than the line. Webster is the full formula, including its empirical correction term. Both formulas over-predict, Harahap more so as the approach nears saturation.

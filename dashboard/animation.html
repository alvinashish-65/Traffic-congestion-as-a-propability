The two formulas share the uniform-delay term R²/(2C(1 − λ/s)), and Harahap's Q(0) is estimated from Webster's overflow term. Webster also subtracts its empirical correction term, which Harahap lacks, so Harahap sits higher, more so near saturation. The running mean still swings from cycle to cycle after warm-up. It needs a few hundred cycles to settle, which is why the R study averages 40 runs of 400 cycles each.
